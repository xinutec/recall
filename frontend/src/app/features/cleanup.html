Cleanup

Long stretches where one mic heard no speech at all. A speech detector has listened to every one of them: a span where it hears a single word is never offered here, whatever the volume says. Biggest first — this list exists to reclaim disk, so it leads with what reclaims the most. A span is not demoted for containing a door closing or someone shifting on the sofa; those are counted next to it (graphic_eq) so you can hear them before deciding, and a span where nothing at all rose above that mic's own floor says so (done_all). Open one to see its waveform, step through its sounds (the priority_high button jumps to the loudest), trim the edges if the detector reached too far, then delete. Deletion is permanent.
