Cleanup

Long stretches where one mic heard nothing but its own noise floor. A speech detector has listened to every one of them: a span where it hears anything at all is never offered here, whatever the volume says. Emptiest first — ranked not by loudness but by how far the audio departs from that mic's own idle noise, so an empty house or a room asleep comes before one where someone was coughing and shifting about. Open a span to see its waveform, step through its sounds (the priority_high button jumps to the loudest), trim the edges if the detector reached too far, then delete. Deletion is permanent.
